KidCam — how it works

  1. Point the camera at the kids. They get cut out of the room and dropped onto a cartoon scene (road / sky / space). The small box top-right shows the raw camera with a dashed box on every face it finds.
  2. Tap a kid's face on the big screen → he's in the vehicle you picked. His head keeps moving inside it as he moves. Moving kid? Hit 📸 Snap to freeze first, then tap.
  3. Drag the vehicle around with a finger. Fling it off the edge (or double-tap it) → he's out, full body again.

Needs: face visible to the camera to lock on. Turning away = tracking pauses (says "where did you go?") and resumes when he looks back.
